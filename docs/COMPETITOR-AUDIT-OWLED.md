# SoloDac vs OWLED Media, 2 November 2026

Compared solodac.com with owledmedia.com on the home page, desktop (1440px) and phone (iPhone 13), with the same measurements for both.

## What OWLED does better

1. Social proof from brands. A wall of known client logos and named testimonials. SoloDac shows its own pages but no client it has worked for.
2. Footage. A video hero and a showreel. SoloDac's hero is type on a gradient; the reels appear further down as covers.
3. Scale and people. "150+ in-house", an office address, a team visual, open roles. SoloDac shows no faces and no team size.
4. A clear structure of divisions with their own names (OWLED Films, OWLED Social, HOOC). SoloDac has the same shape (agency, network, Katran) but it is spread over pages.
5. A share preview image, so links look right on WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X.

## What SoloDac does better

1. Proof with numbers instead of adjectives. OWLED's copy leans on "AI-powered", "moves culture", "breaks through the noise", "clarity in a complex digital world".
2. Speed. About 35 times lighter and 2.5 times faster to load. Matters on Indian mobile networks.
3. A real buying path: what you get, how it works, how we compare, pricing approach, questions, a form.
4. A product (Katran) shown working, not just claimed.
5. Basics: an H1, alt text, security headers, reduced-motion support.

## OWLED's weak spots (avoid copying these)

- All-caps eyebrow labels, highlighter-yellow text and stock "glowing orb" AI illustrations.
- No services page, pricing, process or FAQ; nav only lists sub-brands.
- Phone hero: right-aligned body text over busy video is hard to read.

## What to add to SoloDac, in order

1. Client logos and two or three named testimonials. Needs the owner's client list and quotes.
2. A short muted showreel (10 to 20 seconds) in the home hero or right under it. Needs video files from the owner.
3. Founder and team: a photo of Harshil, team size, the three offices.
4. Share preview image and social meta tags. Can be done now.
5. A careers page or a short open-roles list.
6. Name the three parts on the home page: Agency, Network, Katran.
