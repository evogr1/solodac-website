# Content plan from keyword research

Researched on 5 November 2026 with OpenSEO (India, English). Volumes are searches per month on Google. KD is keyword difficulty, 0 to 100: under 30 is realistic for a new site, over 60 is a fight.

## What the data says

1. **Nobody searches for our exact services.** "Instagram page growth" is 30 a month. "Personal branding for founders" is 20. The jobs we do are real, but the phrases people use are wider and more general.
2. **The best search wins are advice topics.** People search for how to do the thing. We can answer with real numbers from our own pages, which is what most sites cannot do.
3. **"Agency" phrases are small but easy to win.** "Personal branding agency" (590, KD 0), "branding services" (720, KD 7), "Instagram growth services" (590, KD 11) and "Instagram promotion services" (320, KD 0) are all low competition.
4. **City phrases are a real door.** "Branding agency in Bangalore", "Mumbai", "Delhi", "Ahmedabad" each get 700 to 1,300 a month. We have offices in Indore, Chandigarh and Noida, but those cities did not show up in the data, so check them before building pages.

## Small changes to the Services page

These words are already close to what people type. Using them once each in plain sentences helps without stuffing:

- "Instagram growth services" (590, KD 11) in the Instagram section.
- "Personal branding agency" (590, KD 0) in the personal branding section.
- "Branding services" (720, KD 7) in the identity and websites section.

## What not to chase

- "Instagram advertising interests" (27,100) and "instagram ad interests" are about ads, not growth.
- "Youtube charts" (246,000) and "youtube trending" are not our audience.
- Anything with "buy followers" or "buy watch hours". We do not sell that.
